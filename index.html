The scientific question

When measurements disagree, which perturbation effects should a model be trusted to learn?

01 / INTRODUCTION

From noisy experiments to
reproducible evidence.

Multiple perturbation measurements are not interchangeable noisy copies. Acquisition plates, dose conditions, and cross-modal mappings determine which response components remain independently reproducible.

RePert studies this as an estimation, evidence-integration, and downstream-supervision problem—not simply a larger neural network.

🔁01

What is repeatable?

Use independent acquisition plates and matched foreign controls to isolate response signals that generalize to a held-out measurement.

measurement reliability
🧩02

What is complementary?

What actually transfers?

Evaluate when the cleaner response estimate improves morphological agreement or prediction—and when downstream biological tasks do not benefit.

02 / FRAMEWORK

One measured response.
Three complementary estimators.

ReCA combines learned independent-repeat recovery, leave-support-out supervision, and empirical-Bayes shrinkage. Validation-only nonnegative weights integrate their strengths into a repeat-aware response estimate.

🧪 Evaluation design

Support measurements never serve as their own independent evaluation target. Matching uses the compound, dose and acquisition-plate structure. Test outcomes do not select the estimator's weights.

04 / CROSS-MODAL EVIDENCE

More modalities only help
when they add new evidence.

The cross-modal branch updates a frozen target-modality estimate with compound-matched residual evidence from another assay. Shuffled and condition-matched foreign controls test whether the gain depends on the correct pairing.

05 / BIOLOGICAL RELEVANCE

Better estimation is a start.
Not the final endpoint.

RePert follows the information downstream instead of asserting that better repeat agreement must imply better biological decisions. Reported improvements and failure modes should be interpreted together.

↗

Where estimates improve

  • Independent-repeat specificity
  • Morphology-module agreement
  • Several dose-trajectory measures
  • Some downstream response-prediction metrics
≠

Where transfer is not guaranteed

  • No clear general MoA, target or pathway retrieval gain
  • Hit-recovery interval includes zero
  • Direct cross-cell-line estimator transfer may degrade
  • Full upstream training reproduction is not verified
